About this test

Debug test · Dedicated emulator only

Why exercise access?

This compatibility check writes one synthetic exercise session to Health Connect on this test emulator.

What data is used?

The check uses only the test session it creates. It does not read your health history or send this test data to WHOOP or a cloud service.

What happens afterwards?

The check attempts to delete its test session and reports whether cleanup was verified. If it fails, the test record may remain in Health Connect.

Your choice

You can deny this permission or revoke it in Health Connect. Normal workout export stays off in this diagnostic build.

This page explains the test. Permissions are selected in Android's Health Connect screen.
