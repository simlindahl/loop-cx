LOOP Cx · Health Connect

Privacy and data use

Where your data goes

Your recording stays in LOOP Cx's local app storage. When you enable this integration and grant the required access, LOOP Cx writes eligible running sessions, distance and heart-rate samples to Android Health Connect. It does not read other apps' records. This integration does not send your route coordinates, plan or targets to Health Connect, and adds no LOOP Cx server or account.

Other apps you authorise in Health Connect may use the exported records. Writing a run does not confirm WHOOP import or goal credit.

Your choices

Manage permissions and the records stored by Health Connect in Android's Health Connect settings. Other apps' access is controlled there. Turning LOOP Cx export off does not remove records already accepted by Health Connect. JSON sharing is a separate explicit action; its full-file sensitive-data disclosure appears before Android's share sheet.

You can deny or revoke write access and keep using local recording, History and JSON sharing. Turning this integration off stops new export attempts; it does not delete local recordings or records already written to Health Connect.
