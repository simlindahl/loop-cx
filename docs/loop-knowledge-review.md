## Decisions already reflected

Audience: experienced exercisers adapting to career, family, travel and other demands. Explain the complete Core without requiring an app or wearable. Entry under Settings, short introduction, Get started, optional deeper learning. Four-part reading flow. Gym and home choices. Attribution: LOOP by Simon Lindahl. Private reference links and personal race data are excluded.

Core defaults retain two working sets to technical failure, safe stops and recovery adjustments. The original review's single five-effort/38-minute C1 is historical, not the new-start dose. The subsequently approved three-protocol family and shared 4/3 defaults govern the synchronized current reference; frozen historical plans remain unchanged. Optional mobility stays optional. Brand-neutral BLE/HRR requirements remain with their current owners.

The requested refresh explains the interrupted-life assumption, experience/autoregulation, order versus dose, general capacity versus specialization, failure/fatigue, exercise choices and M/recovery compromises. Get started keeps brief why statements; Learn more/system reference carry deeper detail. The new reader has no visible RPE and invents no replacement zone defaults. Its optional Ghost rationale explains personal mastery and contextual self-reference without claiming a health score, compulsory race frequency, leaderboard superiority or complete-programme validation. Ghost runtime rules and [#96](https://github.com/simlindahl/loop-cx/issues/96) target/presentation choices keep their own owners.

Subsequent owner feedback requires a genuinely independent white paper rather than a product-linked summary. The revised [paper](../knowledge/training/system.md) now separates physiology, empirical findings and LOOP's practical choices, with a complete numbered bibliography and near-claim citations. It includes conditioning, strength dose/failure/rest, mixed-work interference, recovery, interrupted exposure and voluntary self-competition, including differing findings across short laboratory studies. It contains no product or Ghost-feature references or outgoing links to product documents; provenance stays in the [migration record](loop-source-migration.md). The reader remains the shorter pedagogical route. Earlier draft approvals do not approve the new prose; fresh independent review and the owner's reading remain required.

## Evidence and editorial limits

Primary sources were checked for resistance training, concurrent training, relative effort, activity recommendations and sleep. References are placed alongside the claims they support in the guide. Research does not validate the exact LOOP system. The guide explicitly separates Core design choices from findings; no silent replacement of the failure default or new weekly quota is proposed.

Home examples are proposed adaptations that preserve movement intent where possible. A safe pulling anchor is a genuine equipment constraint. A row is not presented as identical to a vertical pull. Jumps, mixed work and progression thresholds remain practical Core choices rather than scientifically established minimum doses.
