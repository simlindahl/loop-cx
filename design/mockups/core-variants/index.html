Approved direction

Approved content: SIT 7 × 30 sec all-out / 6 × 3 min recovery, fixed C1 RPE and shared 4/3 min warm-up and cool-down; C2 20–120 minutes in 5-minute steps (45 default)/RPE 4, no arbitrary workout builder.

Approved selection: start with C1.2 selected, then retain the last selected official variant. Change is one compact dialog; Core remains two choices.

Global Settings: one warm-up/cool-down pair overrides all C1 variants for future starts. Existing favourite shortcuts use the current resolved Settings at their next start. Started/past runs keep their frozen plan.

Settings inputs use positive whole minutes to illustrate the preview; final zero/bounds validation is not decided.

C2 uses the approved 20–120-minute slider, in 5-minute steps, with 45 minutes selected initially and fixed RPE 4. A saved C2 shortcut retains its chosen duration and target; C2 never gains warm-up and cool-down. Product limits are guardrails, not evidence that every allowed SIT dose suits every runner.

C1 Personalise allows only the work repetition count and optional pace OR zone, keeping phase lengths and RPE fixed. Main set, including recovery, is limited to 10–60 minutes; warm-up and cool-down is outside this limit. Favourites save an official variant plus chosen count and personal target, not a new protocol or revision number. Work/recovery targets use one chosen mode: pace OR zone. The approved first target form uses this single additional mode.
