Approved direction and test proposals

Compare recent supported comparable pace, not the current split's accumulated mean. A smaller sec/km value is faster. Faster, Steady and Slower are neutral directions, not good/bad performance or a target to chase.

The two adjacent whole 10-second windows, 5 sec/km deadband and cycle = clamp(current sec/km × 4, 900, 1800) ms are uncalibrated demonstration proposals. They are not locked production requirements or accuracy evidence. This additional comparison history is not supplied by the current live snapshot. The existing service-owned live-pace pilot retains its separate policy.

Reset the trend reference after pause, GPS gaps and phase changes; planned recovery is not worse performance. Live pace can be available while the trend acquires support, and short SIT efforts may have no trend. A valid Faster/Slower arrow repeats within 2px horizontally and 3px vertically; current running speed controls its bounded movement speed. Steady stays static. Digits and labels remain stable, without color judgment or repeated live announcements. This is a metaphor, not step frequency or GPS rhythm.

Truthful states and limits

Fresh actual HR may continue pulsing during Manual/Auto pause without implying active time. Invalid/stale/unknown HR shows Unavailable and stops the heart; paused/unavailable pace stops the arrow. Values replace directly, without interpolation. Hidden, pending or reduced-motion states stop effects while retaining truthful static information.

Future app implementation must reuse the existing motion-preference bridge, know the preference before the first effect and after background return, and tear down effects/listeners on unmount. No hidden loop, new data source, RPE change, permission or recording behavior is approved here. Native lifecycle, battery, physical readability and TalkBack need actual QA. The reader runtime hold remains unchanged.
