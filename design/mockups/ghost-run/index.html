The owner approved this concrete twenty-one-state route/reference/start/live/pause/quality/result direction. See the decision and scope. Race execution has no Core/Cx identity, RPE or optional pace/heart-rate-zone targets; measured pace and HR remain measurements.

Route capture and racing are distinct. An eligible completed continuous workout supplies the main route without changing its source history. The illustrated uncertainty/off-route controls, pauses-count warnings and insufficient-GPS save rejection are included.

All routes, dates, distances, times, maps and results are synthetic illustrations. Links move between static examples; no GPS, race clock, recording, permission, map SDK, health write or verification of a real record runs. Android owns exact notification rendering. Other gallery destinations provide context only; the reader runtime hold is unchanged.
