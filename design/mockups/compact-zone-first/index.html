Approved direction · remaining limits

Simon approved this compact hierarchy and disclosure direction on 15 November 2026; see the source-bound decision. Exact zone targets for official variants are not inferred from RPE, and cannot be established by this illustration. A missing sensor never blocks starting or supplies a measured zone.

Scroll remains at large text, with the keyboard or with long errors. No type or target is shrunk to force a one-screen layout. This browser's 200% control is an illustration; native system scaling and TalkBack remain later acceptance.
