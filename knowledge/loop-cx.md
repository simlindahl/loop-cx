# LOOP Cx · official running app for LOOP conditioning workouts
Product direction approved on 12 November 2026. The current visual baseline is [approved revision 5 with neutral Basalt](../design/decision.md#current-decision); earlier revision reviews remain historical in that decision record.
Training reference: [self-contained Core guide](what-is-loop.md), approved as an implementation reference on 13 November 2026 and subsequently revised for the owner-approved C1 variant family. The earlier single C1 revisions remain frozen compatibility records; new Core-first starts use the protocol family described below. [Product specification](product.md) governs app behavior; optional racing plugins remain outside the first knowledge feature.

Core governs training content. The app executes C1/C2 when running is selected. A running-only log cannot determine the next step in the complete C1 → S1 + M → C2 → S2 + M rotation. Keep direct Core starts without a naming requirement and personal shortcuts. Rowing and cycling are valid within Core but outside the running app's current support.
- Historical C1 compatibility: revision 2 uses 8 min progressive warm-up, 5 × 3 min controlled hard effort, 2 min easy movement between efforts and 4 min easy finish. Exactly 35 min total and 23 min main. This owner-approved official programme update also applies to the training guide; prior revision 1 plans retain their 10/5 warm-up and cool-down and 38-minute total. Global C1 warm-up and cool-down defaults can be overridden in Settings for new resolved/frozen plans, not per-run options. Core contains an inconsistent reference to four intervals; use the table's five in the template. Avoid low-heart-rate warnings that increase pace early in a work phase. Use RPE as the primary target. Template RPE values are suggested app guidelines, not exact Core rules.
- Historical C2 compatibility: a source-labelled zone 2 favourite, normally 45–60 min. Simon approved revision 1 defaults on 13 November 2026: exactly one 45-minute work phase, target RPE 3 as a suggested app guideline, and no separate warm-up or cool-down. The 45 minutes is both main-set and total active time for this initial template; duration remains adjustable. Let zone/conversational effort govern; Zone 2 is target intent until actual heart-rate integration supplies measurements. The superseded generic 3/2 warm-up and cool-down proposal is not a current Core-first rule; C2 remains without warm-up and cool-down.
- Show the official variant purpose before start, preserving template source/revision and the actual resolved plan with the execution. Core-first shortcuts save the permitted targets and selected C2 duration without becoming arbitrary custom workouts. Legacy classifications remain frozen with their original records; do not relabel history as a new official variant.
- 10K plugin: optional. Most time intervals can be expressed. Weeks 9–10 include a 3 × 2 km option with 2–3 min jogging, which conflicts with the approved shared unit. The plugin's 3 × 8 min alternative can be expressed. Introduce separate phase units only after deciding to support the complete plugin. Its C1 specifies 10–15 min easy warm-up and cool-down jogging. Do not automate weekly quotas.

The [C1 variant purposes and evidence](running-workouts.md#c1-variant-purposes-and-evidence) records the subsequently approved three-protocol direction and fixed RPE values, research support and remaining delivery/design decisions. These protocols govern new Core-first starts without relabelling or replacing existing frozen C1 revisions.

The subsequent Core-first C2 programme uses a 20–120-minute slider in five-minute steps, default 45, target RPE 4 and no warm-up and cool-down. New template revision 2 distinguishes this from the earlier revision 1 / RPE 3; actual started plans and historical runs remain frozen. This approved programme change is specified with the new Core-first flows and is resolved by the Core-first runtime; it does not rewrite historical records.

The approved C1 rep control keeps work/recovery durations and RPE fixed while allowing integer counts whose main set, including recovery, is 10–60 minutes. Default counts are 7/5/3; saved shortcuts retain selected counts. This supersedes the earlier fixed-count-only interpretation, not the Core-first boundary against a general workout builder.
